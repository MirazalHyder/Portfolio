My story

Where it began, and what it cost

My journey began in 2017 with a question that would not leave me alone: could a robot stand beside a person on the worst day of their life? The Helping Robot I built, designed to guide people to safety and call for help, was only a demonstration, yet it taught me that engineering is most meaningful when it protects people.

Since then I have learned that building is mostly persistence. Chassis flex, sensors misread, motors stall, and a design that looked flawless on screen can fail on its first run. I now return to the bench the next morning.

On the Merch Rover project I carried that mindset into a team working under real pressure. Finishing 1st in Asia and 3rd globally was proof that discipline, trust and endurance build winning machines far more reliably than talent alone.
